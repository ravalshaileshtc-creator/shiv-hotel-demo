Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 07, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use and disclosure of Your information when You use the Shiv Hotel smart restaurant ordering application (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide a smooth digital concierge and dining ordering experience. This includes:

  • User Profile Data: Name and contact phone number to sync cart orders and compute loyalty points.
  • Location & Table Context: Scanned table codes to ensure orders are served to the correct physical location.
  • Audio Permissions: Voice recognition inputs for the AI Chef features. Spoken commands are converted to text locally using native browser speech recognition engines and are not permanently stored or recorded on our servers.

2. Use of Your Personal Data

The Company may use Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our Service, including to monitor the usage of our Service.
  • To manage Your Account: to manage Your registration as a user of the Service.
  • To contact You: By phone calls or SMS regarding updates or informative communications.
  • To manage Your orders: to fulfill and manage your table food orders.

3. Security of Your Personal Data

The security of Your Personal Data is important to Us. We use industry-standard Firestore database security rules to isolate tenant data and secure active connections.

4. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update Our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify You of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
